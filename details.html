Midnight Horizon (2025)

Sci-Fi Adventure PG-13 ⭐ 8.4 / 10

Plot Synopsis

In the year 2147, a crew of explorers aboard the research vessel Aurora detects a mysterious signal from beyond the edge of the solar system. As they travel toward the source, they discover that the signal is a message from humanity's own future, and that time is running out to change what is about to happen.
